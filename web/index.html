How to Use SignSpeak

SignSpeak empowers mute and speech-impaired individuals to communicate naturally by recognizing hand signs through the camera and converting them to text on screen.

  1. Show your hand: Place your hand in front of the camera inside the guide box. The 21 cyan landmark points track your finger joints at full camera frame rate.
  2. Hold the sign: When your gesture is recognized, hold it steady for ~0.8 second. The green progress ring fills up and plays a confirmation chime when accepted.
  3. Avoid repeats: Once captured, the letter is locked so it won't repeat accidentally. Relax your hand or switch signs to continue.
  4. Word Suggestions: Tap any suggested word or phrase to autocomplete it and insert a space.
  5. Quick Phrases: Tap any common emergency phrase button at the bottom for instant insertion.
